쓰는 정보와 이유

  • 댓글 — @oh.haedal 게시물에 달린 댓글의 번호 · 글 · 작성자 이름. 댓글로 자료(링크)를 요청한 분에게 그 링크를 비공개 메시지로 한 번 보내 드리는 데만 씁니다.
  • 메시지 — @oh.haedal 로 온 메시지와 버튼 누름. 요청에 답하는 데만 씁니다.
  • 팔로우 여부 — 메시지의 버튼을 누른 분이 @oh.haedal 을 팔로우하는지. 팔로우하신 분께 링크를 보내 드리는 데만 씁니다.
  • 게시물 — 운영자가 만든 게시물을 @oh.haedal 에 올리고, 올린 게시물의 댓글 수를 확인합니다.

Privacy Policy

oh.haedal (Instagram @oh.haedal) · Effective October 27, 2026

What this app does

The oh.haedal app is a personal tool that manages the operator's own Instagram professional account, @oh.haedal. It publishes the operator's posts and responds to comments on @oh.haedal posts and to messages sent to @oh.haedal. It is not used for any other account and is not offered to other users.

Information it uses and why

  • Comments — the ID, text and author username of comments on @oh.haedal posts, used only to send a requested link once, as a private reply, to the person who asked for it in a comment.
  • Messages — messages and button taps sent to @oh.haedal, used only to respond to the request.
  • Follow status — whether a person who tapped a button in our message follows @oh.haedal, used only to send the link to followers.
  • Media — publishing the operator's own posts to @oh.haedal and checking their comment counts.

Storage and sharing

  • To avoid sending twice, only the comment ID, author username and send status are recorded on the operator's computer. The app has no server.
  • Records of people waiting for a follow check are deleted after 7 days.
  • The operator may receive a notification (author username and text) when a new comment or message arrives.
  • Information is never transferred or sold to anyone, and is not used for advertising, analytics or AI training.

Deletion requests

To have your records deleted, follow the data deletion instructions.
